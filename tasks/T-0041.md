## Goal

(`useDockspaceMediaController.ts`, gallery and duplicates), "1 items selected for trash." (Find duplicates footer) and "This will move 1 selected media files to trash." (trash dialog) in `DockspaceMediaWorkspace.tsx`. 1. Singular and plural are correct in the trash result ("Moved 1 item to trash." / "Moved N items to trash.", including the "M failed." variant), the duplicates footer ("1 item selected for trash.") and the dialog description ("This will move 1 selected media file to trash.").
2. A successful result in Find duplicates shows in the neutral info style, like the gallery's; a result with failed items may stay in the error style.
3. Unit tests cover singular and plural text and the info tone. Build, typecheck and tests pass.
