# T-0038 Phone touch targets at least 44px: uploads pill and gallery selection bar
Issue: #82
Request: #65
Origin: T-0030
Status: done

## Goal
On phone (900px wide or less), make the remaining small touch targets at least 44px tall: the collapsed uploads pill (`apps/web/src/components/files/UploadsCard.tsx`, about 33px today, used by the files page and the media gallery) and the gallery selection bar buttons (Cancel, Add to album, Trash selected, about 30px today with `ui-button--sm`). The T-0030 final design (section 7) asks for 44px targets on phone; the album button, ⋯, the Upload pill and album rows already meet it. Found while testing T-0030 (#67), round 1, finding 4.

## Acceptance criteria
1. On phone, the collapsed uploads pill is at least 44px tall on both the files page and the media gallery, and its label, failure dot and position next to the Upload pill are unchanged.
2. On phone, the gallery selection bar buttons (Cancel, Add to album, Trash selected) are at least 44px tall, and the two-row selection bar still fits at 390px wide without horizontal scroll.
3. Desktop sizes are unchanged. Visible focus stays on every changed control, and there are no new axe violations.
4. Tests updated where they check sizes or classes. Build, typecheck and tests pass.
