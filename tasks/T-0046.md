# T-0046 Files page phone Upload button at least 44px, matching the uploads pill
Issue: #98
Request: #65
Origin: T-0038
Status: new

## Goal
On phone (900px wide or less), the floating Upload button at the bottom of the files page (`.dockspace-upload-menu--floating .dockspace-upload-menu__trigger` in `apps/web/src/styles/layout.css`) is about 37px tall. That is under the 44px phone touch target the T-0030 design (section 7) asks for, and after T-0038 it no longer matches the 44px collapsed uploads pill beside it (tops differ by 6.6px; on the gallery both are 44px). Found while testing T-0038 (#82), round 1, finding 1.

## Acceptance criteria
1. On phone, the files page's floating Upload button is at least 44px tall, and the collapsed uploads pill beside it lines up with it (same top and bottom), in both the in-progress and failed pill states.
2. The Upload menu still opens above the button and its items keep working; label, icon and position are otherwise unchanged; no horizontal scroll at 390px wide.
3. Desktop sizes are unchanged. Visible focus stays on the button, and there are no new axe violations.
4. The T-0038 CSS comment for the files-page pill ("matches the 44px Upload pill beside it") is accurate afterwards. Tests updated where they check sizes or classes (for example `apps/web/src/tests/styles/phoneTouchTargets.test.ts`). Build, typecheck and tests pass.
